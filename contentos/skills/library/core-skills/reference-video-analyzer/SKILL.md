# Reference Video Analyzer

1. Verify access to the supplied URL or file. State clearly if the media, transcript, or captions are unavailable.
2. Extract timestamped beats, shot changes, composition, motion, typography, transitions, pacing, audio cues, and narration-to-visual relationships.
3. Separate general techniques from distinctive protected expression. Do not reproduce exact frames, scripts, characters, or shot sequences.
4. Create a reusable style card and storyboard.
5. Map each technique to existing factory capabilities and skills.
6. Identify gaps and propose the smallest safe prototype.
7. Preserve source URLs and notes; do not claim to have watched inaccessible media.
8. Do not download or use copyrighted media assets unless rights and use are appropriate.
